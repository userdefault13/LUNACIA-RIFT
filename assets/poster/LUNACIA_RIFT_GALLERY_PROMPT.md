# Lunacia Rift — gallery poster prompt

Marketing thumbnail only. Not the match UI. Not a tilemap. Paste into your own image tool. This sheet is not finished art.

Master: 1920×1080 (16:9). It must still read at about 640×360. Keep the wordmark and the three creature silhouettes inside the center 80% so a card crop does not clip them.

## Direction

Titled key art, not a HUD. One jungle rift, three lanes, three creatures. Saturated class colors on a dark ground. Big wordmark. No health bars, minimap, ability icons, or scoreboard.

Pitch to show, not to typeset as a paragraph: a 1v1 three-lane fight, one creature per lane, the fight ends at the enemy Nest.

Do not trace or lift an official Axie character sheet. Invent three original creatures that read the roster roles: plant hold, beast tempo, aquatic finish.

## Prompt

Competition key art poster, 16:9, for a 1v1 three-lane micro-MOBA called Lunacia Rift. A lush Lunacia jungle split by a glowing rift, three dirt lanes running left to right through deep green canopy, readable as three paths even at thumbnail size. Foreground: three original cute-fierce creatures, one per lane, large silhouettes, not a screenshot. Left lane: a sturdy plant creature, leaf and pumpkin shell, color #3d9e4a, holding the lane. Center lane: a lean beast, horn and claws, color #c45a1a, mid-charge. Right lane: a round aquatic creature, splash and fin, color #2a7ec4, about to finish. Night sky and ground #0d1218, jungle floor #152018, rim light in title ice #7ec8ff. Huge custom wordmark “LUNACIA RIFT” across the top third, bold, high contrast, no other text. Saturated, painterly game-poster finish, clear shapes, no UI chrome.
