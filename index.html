How to play

Goal

Each side has three lanes guarded by Spires. Break any two inner Spires to unlock the enemy Nest, then destroy it.

Your Axies

You command Buba (hold), Olek (tempo) and Puffy (finish), one per lane. The two you are not controlling fight on lane AI.

Gold

Last-hit Packs and destroy structures for gold. Spend it in the Shop, or at your Dens and Spires.

Genes = skills

Mouth, Horn, Back, Tail → Q W E R. Classes counter each other: Plant > Beast > Aquatic > Plant.
